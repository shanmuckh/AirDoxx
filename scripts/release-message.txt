AirDoxx 2.3: Wi-Fi roaming, lid close/open, phone switcher, custom shortcuts, CI

- Keeps a Wi-Fi adb link (port 5555) alongside Wireless debugging, which Android
  switches off when the phone leaves its network; finds the phone again at its
  new address (mDNS, saved addresses, hotspot gateway)
- Lid closed / Mac sleeps: mirroring stops and the phone gets its screen back;
  on wake AirDoxx reconnects by itself
- A lost wireless phone no longer dead-ends: AirDoxx keeps looking and resumes
- Hardening: detects a phone that stopped answering, ends the session when the
  control channel dies, asks for a key frame after a decoder reset, bounds
  packet sizes, skips private/oversized clipboard items
- Switch Phone menu and Switch to Next Phone; remembers the phone used last
- Keyboard Shortcuts window: remap the phone, capture and window commands
- OTP auto-copy, battery/signal in the title bar, screen recording, keep on top
- GitHub Actions: builds the universal DMG on every push; attaches it on a tag
- Website: hosted install.sh (curl | bash), "New in 2.3" section, new tiles
- Version 2.3.0
